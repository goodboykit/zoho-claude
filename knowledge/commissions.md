# Commissions basics

**What it is:** extra pay salespeople earn for selling. A commissions platform tracks sales, applies the pay rules, and shows everyone what they earned.

## Words you'll hear
- **Quota:** the sales target (e.g. $100,000 per quarter).
- **Attainment:** how much of the quota they hit. `sold ÷ quota` (80,000 ÷ 100,000 = 80%).
- **Rate:** the percent they earn (e.g. 10% of deal amount).
- **Tiers / accelerators:** higher rate after a point (10% up to quota, 15% above).
- **Cap:** maximum payout.
- **Split:** two reps share credit (60/40).
- **Clawback / chargeback:** taking commission back if the customer cancels or doesn't pay.
- **SPIFF:** a short-term bonus for a specific product or goal.
- **Draw:** an advance paid before commission is earned.
- **Period close:** the date numbers are locked for payment.

## Example calculation
Quota $100k, sold $120k, 10% up to quota, 15% above:

- **Step 1.** First $100k × 10% = $10,000
- **Step 2.** Extra $20k × 15% = $3,000
- **Step 3.** Total = **$13,000**

## How a platform spans the Zoho tools
`CRM (deals) → Creator (plans, rules, adjustments, approvals) → Analytics (calculations & dashboards) → statements to reps`

Varicent can replace or sit beside the calculation part.

## Watch out
- Every number must trace back to deals. Keep an audit trail (who changed what, when).
- Rules change each year; store plans by period, never overwrite old ones.
- Reconcile totals before paying.
